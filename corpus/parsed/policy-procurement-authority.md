## PROCUREMENT AUTHORITY AND APPROVAL LIMITS

Effective 15 June 2025

## 1. Store manager

May approve a purchase order up to INR 40,000 against a supplier with terms in force.

## 2. Owner

Required for any order above INR 40,000, any order against a supplier with no terms in force, and any order placed outside the agreed lead time.

## 3. Automated drafting

A system may draft a purchase order. It may not place one. Every draft expires if not approved, and re-validates its prices before execution.
