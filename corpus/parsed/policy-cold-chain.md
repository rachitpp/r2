## COLD CHAIN AND PERISHABLEHANDLING

Effective 01September2025

## 1.Dairy

Received between 2°C and 6°C. Any consignment outside that range at the point of receipt is refused entire.

## 2.Fresh produce

Inspected on receipt; wastage above 5% of the consignment is raised as a return the same day.

## 3. Recording

Receiving temperature is written on the delivery note and retained with the invoice.
